# Code of Conduct

Task Trunk App uses a simple contributor standard: be respectful, specific, and
constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about problems.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect user privacy and avoid sharing private ticket data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or repeated low-effort comments.
- Dismissing bug reports without attempting to understand them.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that make the
project harder to maintain or unsafe for contributors.
